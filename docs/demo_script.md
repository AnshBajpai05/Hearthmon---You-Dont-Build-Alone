# Hearthmon — 90-Second Demo Script

For a recruiter / showcase. Goal: they walk away feeling *"this is intentional and expensive,"*
not *"cool student project."* Restraint sells this — let silences breathe; don't over-talk.

---

## ⏱ Prep (do 2 min before they sit down)
So the "memory" + "it noticed" beats fire live instead of being explained:

1. **Renderer = Alive** — press **V** so the premium habitat is showing. Place the window somewhere clean.
2. **Pre-seed one memory** — a day or two earlier (or now, then change the clock isn't needed — just have history): press **M**, log a **low / stressed** mood with a tiny note. This lets *"we've been here before"* surface a real past moment, not a platitude.
3. **Point it at a repo** — open the coding panel, set a local git folder you can commit to. Keep a terminal ready in that repo.
4. Have your companion be one with a gorgeous habitat (water/ghost/dragon biome screenshots best).

> If anything won't fire live, **narrate it** ("normally this happens when…") — never poke at it twice.

---

## 🎬 The five moments (~90s)

**1 · Presence (0:00–0:15)**
*Do:* nothing. Let it sit, breathe, maybe wander. Pet it once (hearts rise). Drag it across the screen.
*Say:* "It's not a productivity app or a chatbot. It just lives beside your work. The whole point is
that it **doesn't** demand anything — it notices *how* you build, not *that* you did."

**2 · Two skins, one soul (0:15–0:30)**
*Do:* press **V** → Classic, then **V** → Alive again.
*Say:* "Same emotional brain underneath — two renderers. A cozy one, and this." (let the habitat land.)

**3 · It noticed my work (0:30–0:50)** — *the "wait, what?" beat*
*Do:* in your terminal, `git commit --allow-empty -m "fix the off-by-one"` → the pet reacts with a
spoken commit/fix line.
*Say:* "It's watching the work itself — commits, fixes, long training runs — from privacy-safe
signals. Never keystrokes, never my screen, never my code." (beat) "And it's **100% local** — nothing
ever leaves the machine."

**4 · Memory over motivation (0:50–1:10)** — *the emotional turn*
*Do:* press **M**, log today's mood. The *"we've been here before"* line surfaces the seeded past one.
*Say:* "Most tools hand you a generic 'you got this.' This hands you back **your own** history —
'back then you felt this too, and you're still here.' That's memory, not motivation."

**5 · The journey (1:10–1:30)** — *the closer*
*Do:* open the radial menu (**✦** on the edge) → **Journey**. Show the timeline + **bond depth**
(Stranger → … → Lifetime Companion).
*Say:* "Everything's a scrapbook it's quietly kept — and the bond actually deepens over real time and
real check-ins. After a month, it's not the same companion you started with."

**Closer (one line, then stop talking):**
> "It's built to feel like building *with someone* — not being managed by software."

## 🧠 If they ask (this is the unfakeable part — copycats can clone the repo, not these answers)

- **"Why notice effort, not emotion?"** — Emotion-detection is invasive and usually wrong. Effort —
  flow, friction, breakthrough — is inferrable from safe signals (foreground app *names*, commit
  cadence) and is what actually deserves witnessing. You honor the work, not a guessed feeling.
- **"Why 100% local?"** — Trust. The moment a companion that "watches you work" phones home, it's
  surveillance. Local-first is the entire reason it's allowed near your real workflow.
- **"How does Chapter Memory work, psychologically?"** — It records *effort density* and *survived
  hard days*, then resurfaces them weeks later — because resilience you can't remember doesn't help
  you. Memory > motivation.
- **"Is this AI / an LLM?"** — No LLM required; it's a scripted emotional engine (state + memory in
  local SQLite). An optional Claude/Ollama layer is future, opt-in. The restraint is deliberate —
  the warmth comes from memory and timing, not a chatbot.
- **"Why Pokémon?"** — Nostalgia does the heavy lifting on attachment; the sprite layer is swappable
  for original art if it ever goes public.

---

## 🙅 Don't demo (cut for time — *mention*, don't show)
Battle Arena, evolution ceremony, the music-vibe reactions, Ash-throw voice. One sentence each if
asked: "it also does X." Showing them eats your 90 seconds and dilutes the emotional arc.

## ✦ Optional flex (authorship, for placement disputes)
Click the faint signature woven into the habitat → the signed **provenance** panel (founder, build,
commit, signature). "Every build is cryptographically signed to me." Quietly powerful; don't dwell.
