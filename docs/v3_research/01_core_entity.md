# 01 — Core Entity

> **Central question: what is the thing being preserved?** Before any schema, decide *conceptually*
> what an entry *is*. Do not write a table until this is clear.

## The candidates

| Candidate | What it means |
|---|---|
| People | Sanctuary |
| Pets | Companionship |
| Places | Nostalgia |
| Objects | Identity markers |
| Projects | Builder history |
| Characters / fiction | Comfort |
| Moments | Life milestones |

## Hypotheses to test (do NOT pick yet)

1. **One entity, many types.** They're all "a thing that shaped you / brings you peace," with a
   light *type* tag. Simplest. The "why" is the only thing that's truly required.
2. **Two entities, two axes.** *Shaped me* (identity) and *gives me peace* (refuge) behave
   differently and deserve different treatment. These two axes already map onto two real
   literatures — **continuing bonds** (people/loss) and **transitional objects** (comfort/refuge).
   See [07](07_sources_and_influences.md).
3. **An entity vs. an event.** Is a *Moment* (graduation) a first-class entity, or a *relationship/
   event* attached to entities? That bleeds into [02](02_relationship_graphs.md).
4. **The atom is a *scene*, not an entity.** Memory stores moments that hold *several* entities at
   once (*"Saturday mornings watching Pokémon with my brother at grandma's house"*), not isolated
   cards — so an entry may need to hold **multiple entities + a feeling**, and a single-entity card
   is just the simplest scene. *Reconciliation to test:* the user **places single things** (low
   friction, scarce — [03](03_memory_rituals.md)) and **scenes/constellations crystallize** from how
   those things co-occur and connect — *you don't author scenes, they assemble.* Keeps placement
   simple while letting structure become brain-like. See [02](02_relationship_graphs.md).

## Conceptual data-model questions (no schema, just shape)

- What is the **minimum viable entity**? (Working guess: a name + a *why*. Everything else optional.)
- Is **type** required, or inferred/optional metadata? (Doctrine says: never the leading question.)
- Can the same real-world thing be two entries with two different *whys*? (The keyboard problem.)
- Does an entity have *state* (Living/Resting/…) from day one, or is that a later layer?

## What would close this question

Real first entries from V2-era testers + the broadened shelf experiment. Until people place things,
this stays a thought experiment. **Do not decide from the armchair.**
