# 05 — Presence Models (research only)

> **Central question: what is a Presence?** This is where "I Choose You" *eventually* belongs — but
> **not now. Research only. Choose nothing yet.**

## Candidates (do NOT pick)

- portrait
- keepsake
- spirit
- memory echo
- symbolic creature
- constellation

## What we already hold (from doctrine)

- Presence models differ by relationship: **living** = present/reactive; **mentor** = appears at
  crossroads; **passed** = rare, *encountered*, never summoned, never "played with."
- The user **picks** the Presence from a small set the traits merely *rank*; the system never decrees.
- Generate **once**, store **forever**, never silently re-roll — consistency *is* the bond.
- **Presence > conversation** (SOUL): a Presence should mostly do *nothing*. It may never need to speak.

## The dangerous frontier: do not sleepwalk into a griefbot

The moment a "Presence" represents someone who **passed**, you are near *digital-afterlife /
reanimation* territory — ethically fraught, and easy to get horribly wrong. Carry the Gita's line in
([04](04_emotional_safety.md)) as a hard constraint:

> A Presence is a **symbol of what endures** (warmth, a lesson, a steadiness) — **never an
> impersonation** of the person. Impersonation is clinging to the form; that is the suffering the
> whole design is trying to avoid.

So *constellation* (a quiet pattern of light that appears on an anniversary) may be far safer than
*creature* (a pet you interact with) for the deceased. Study this before choosing anything.

## Strong prior art to study

Cultures have honored the absent **without reanimating them** for centuries — Shinto *kamidana* /
*Obon*, ancestor veneration, the Vietnam Veterans Memorial (presence through *names*, through absence).
These are 1,000-year-old solutions to the exact problem. Don't reinvent; learn. See [07](07_sources_and_influences.md).

## Open questions

- Must a Presence ever speak? (Default answer, per SOUL: no.)
- Is a Presence *generated*, *chosen from a set*, or *grown* from accumulated memories?
- Does a Presence for a living person and a passed person share *any* code path, or are they separate
  by design so the sacred one can never be accidentally gamified?
