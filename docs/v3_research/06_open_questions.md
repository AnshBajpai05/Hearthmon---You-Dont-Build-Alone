## Closed — rejected for now (don't re-propose without a genuinely new reason)

These keep re-arriving as "new" ideas. Writing the **no** down is half the value.

- ❌ **Ghibli (or any) generation engine** — brand / anti-AI landmine + months fighting visuals; all generation is deferred.
- ❌ **Webscraping images** — inconsistent (an entry must not change face), copyright-messy, and pulls focus to *assets* over *meaning*.
- ❌ **Automatic memory blending** — confabulation; betrays a truth-keeping vault. ([04](04_emotional_safety.md): *associate, never confabulate.*)
- ❌ **Franchise-asset-first design** (e.g. lead with a PokeAPI sprite) — recognition *without* meaning; the *story* matters, the sprite doesn't. A recognition anchor for a comfort entry is a *Stage-3* maybe, never the start.
- ❌ **Folder-style "spaces"** — folders → organizing → administration → dead magic. Use emergent *constellations* instead.

> Note the lanes (your framing): **Soul / Doctrine** and **Product Architecture** ideas are *explored*
> here; **Implementation** ideas (engines, scraping, APIs, portrait pipelines) are *out of scope until
> V2 has real feedback and the doctrine is clear.* Don't evaluate the three together.
