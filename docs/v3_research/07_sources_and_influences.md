# 07 — Sources & Influences

> An **annotated reading list** to grow over time — blogs, papers, books, traditions, and design
> prior art. Add links as you find them. These are *starting threads*, not authorities; go to
> primary sources, and hold every one with humility.

## The two anchor literatures (start here)

- **Continuing Bonds** — Klass, Silverman & Nickman, *Continuing Bonds: New Understandings of Grief*
  (1996). The thesis that healthy grief is a *transformed, ongoing bond*, not detachment. The single
  most relevant academic anchor — it scientifically supports the sanctuary and reframes "Release."
- **Transitional objects** — D. W. Winnicott, *Playing and Reality* (1971). The childhood blanket,
  the comfort character — the psychology of the *refuge* axis (Totoro, Pikachu, the worn book).

## Attachment & bonding (psychology)

- John Bowlby & Mary Ainsworth — *attachment theory*; secure vs. anxious attachment. Directly relevant
  to the SOUL rule *never foster dependency* — design for **secure** attachment, not anxious clinging.
- Research on *nostalgia* (Sedikides, Wildschut) — nostalgia as a resource for meaning and resilience.

## Philosophy of attachment & impermanence

- **Bhagavad Gita** — esp. Ch. 2 (*atman* is eternal; grief for the form is misplaced),
  *nishkama karma* (act without attachment to the fruits), *sthitaprajna* (steady wisdom).
  The tension and reconciliation: **hold close without clinging**, see [04](04_emotional_safety.md).
  *Translations/commentaries to read:* Eknath Easwaran (accessible); Gavin Flood & Charles Martin
  (scholarly); Winthrop Sargeant (word-by-word). Don't rely on a single rendering.
- **Stoicism** — Epictetus, *Enchiridion* 11 ("never say you have lost anything, only that you returned
  it"); *memento mori*; the loved as *on loan*. Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations*.
- **Buddhism** — *anicca* (impermanence), *anatta* (non-self), non-attachment ≠ cold detachment.
- **Japanese aesthetics** — *mono no aware* (the gentle ache of transience), *wabi-sabi*, and
  **kintsugi** (mending a break with gold so the history becomes the beauty) — a near-perfect metaphor
  for *Release*: the rupture is honored, not erased.

## Honoring the absent without reanimating them (prior art for Presence)

- Shinto *kamidana* / *Obon*; ancestor veneration practices — centuries-old "presence without
  impersonation."
- Memorial design — Maya Lin's Vietnam Veterans Memorial (presence through *names* and absence).

## Design & product references

- **Finch**, **Animal Crossing** — compassion without cringe; calm companionship, no loss/punishment
  (already in [EMOTIONAL_SAFETY](../EMOTIONAL_SAFETY.md)).
- **StoryCorps** — the ritual of recording *why someone matters*; the "Great Questions" list. Core to
  [03](03_memory_rituals.md).
- **1 Second Everyday**, **Day One** — memory capture done well (and where it goes flat).
- **We Croak** — a death-awareness app: study what it gets right *and* the line it walks. Cautionary.

## Tech-ethics cautions (read before any "Presence" of the deceased)

- Carl Öhman, *The Afterlife of Data* — the ethics of the digital dead.
- The "**griefbot** / deadbot" literature — why simulating a lost person is fraught. Hard guardrail
  for [05](05_presence_models.md).
